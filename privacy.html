Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy covers Downwind: the DownwindTracker iPhone and Apple Watch app, the web app at app.downwindtracker.com, and the Downwind Cloud backend behind both ("Downwind", "we"). The app and the web app use the same account and the same data.

Plain summary: When you start a paddle session, the Apple Watch records GPS and motion sensor data. You sign in with your email to upload it for analysis, and the sessions and results are stored with your account. To write your coaching report, we send your session metrics, your notes about the session, and your profile record (including your email address) to an AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic). Heart rate and energy from Apple Health stay on your devices and in Health; we don't upload them. We don't sell your data, run ads, or put analytics or tracking SDKs in the app or the web app.

1. Data we collect

Category What When
Account Your email address (for magic-link sign-in) and an account ID (UUID). We also keep a baseline paddling speed worked out from your sessions. When you sign in and when sessions are analyzed.
Location GPS position, altitude, speed, course, and accuracy values, about once per second. Only while a session is recording on the watch.
Motion Accelerometer readings at about 100 per second, plus device-motion data at about 100 per second: rotation rate (gyroscope), acceleration with gravity removed, and watch orientation. We use these to detect strokes and pieces. The app doesn't use the compass. Only while a session is recording on the watch.
Apple Health With your permission, the watch app reads heart rate and active and resting energy during a session, and saves the session to Health as a paddling workout. The saved workout includes your GPS route, paddle distance, average and top speed, heart rate, calories, and the Downwind session ID. Heart rate is shown live on the watch. We don't upload heart rate or energy data from Health to our servers. During and at the end of a recording session.
Files you upload Activity files from other devices (for example Garmin .fit files, or .csv files) that you upload in the web app. These can contain GPS, heart rate, and device details, and we analyze what they contain, including heart rate. When you upload a file.
Session details Session ID, start time, duration, sample counts and rates, file checksums, piece start/end markers, and anything you add about a session: craft, seat and crew notes, session type, focus, conditions (wind, swell, tide, current), and free-text notes. Generated automatically; the details you add are optional.
Diagnostics Apple's crash reports and performance data, if you've turned on sharing in iOS Settings. When something fails on our backend, an error report goes to Sentry (see section 4). The app itself has no third-party crash or analytics SDK. When an error happens, or if enabled on your device.

What we don't collect

  • Contacts, photos, or files other than the session recordings and the activity files you choose to upload.
  • Microphone or camera input.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA). We don't use ad networks.
  • Analytics or tracking: no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or Segment in the app or the web app.

2. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To store and analyze your sessions: bump detection, dead-water sections, stroke analysis, and session metrics.
  • To write an AI coaching report for each session (see section 4).
  • To show your session history and results in the app and the web app.
  • To find and fix bugs, including the problems you report to us.
  • To improve the analysis. We may look at aggregate, de-identified statistics for this. We never use an individual session for marketing or advertising.

3. Where your data is kept and how it travels

  • On your devices: Recordings are saved on the Apple Watch, sent to your iPhone over WatchConnectivity, and kept in the app's storage until you upload or delete them. Sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. The web app keeps your sign-in session in your browser's storage.
  • In transit: Everything goes over HTTPS. The iOS app also uses certificate pinning when it talks to our API.
  • On our servers: Your account, session files, and analysis results are stored in Supabase (database, sign-in, and file storage, in the United States). Our API and the web app run on Fly.io, also in the United States.

4. Who we share data with

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal data. These service providers handle data for us, and only to run Downwind:

  • Supabase, Inc. · Sign-in, database, and file storage. Holds your email, account ID, session files, and analysis results.
  • Fly.io (Fly, Inc.) · Hosts our API and the web app. Your data passes through Fly's servers when you upload, view, or analyze sessions.
  • OpenAI or Anthropic · Writes your AI coaching report. We use one of the two at a time and may switch between them. For each analyzed session we send: the session's metrics (speeds, distances, bumps, dead-water sections, stroke and heart-rate figures where available), its start time, comparisons with your earlier sessions, what you entered about the session (session type, focus, conditions, and your notes), and your profile record, which includes your email address and baseline speed. We don't send your GPS track or raw sensor files.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) · Error reports from our backend. A report includes the error details, the request path and method, and your account ID. It doesn't include what you uploaded or typed, your sign-in tokens, or your email address.
  • Esri · Map images in the web app. Your browser loads them from Esri's servers, so Esri sees your IP address and the map area you're viewing.
  • Google Fonts · Fonts for this website and the web app. Your browser loads them from Google, so Google sees your IP address.
  • Apple Inc. · App Store and TestFlight distribution, Apple Health on your devices, and crash reports if you've turned them on.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of users or the public.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your sessions until you delete your account. Deleting a session in the app or the web app removes it from your history and results straight away, but its files and analysis stay on our servers until your account is deleted.

To delete your account and all of its data, email support@downwindtracker.com from the address you sign in with. We'll process the request within 30 days.

Recordings that haven't been uploaded are removed when you delete the app. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you can ask to see, correct, export, or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email us to do any of these. Using these rights won't affect how we treat you.

7. Children

Downwind isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. International transfers

Downwind is run from the United States, and our servers are there. If you use it from another country, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States.

9. Security

We use HTTPS for all connections, certificate pinning in the iOS app, the iOS Keychain for sign-in tokens, and the security controls of Supabase and Fly.io. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you as the law requires.

10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For bigger changes we'll also tell you in the app or by email.
